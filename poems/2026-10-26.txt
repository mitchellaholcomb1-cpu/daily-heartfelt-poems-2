In a world that rushes past, your grace is where I rest,
a quiet reminder of everything good and true.
I watch the selfless warmth you give without a thought,
and wonder how I ever walked the earth before knowing you.
When the shadows lean in and your spirit starts to tire,
remember the fierce, bright strength you keep inside.
You have moved mountains with your gentle, patient heart,
and I will stand beside you through every turning tide.
Take a breath, my love, and let yourself just be—
you are deeply cherished, and always enough for me.
