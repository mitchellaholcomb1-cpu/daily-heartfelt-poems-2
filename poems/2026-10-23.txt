The gentle way you carry so much grace
Can turn a heavy day to quiet light.
I love the warmth that lives upon your face,
And how you make a weary world feel right.
Whenever doubt begins to cast its shade
And you forget the courage you possess,
Remember all the beauty you have made
With just your patience and your tenderness.
I am so grateful just to walk beside you,
To witness all the goodness that you share;
Wherever this unfolding life may guide you,
You are so deeply loved, and fiercely rare.
