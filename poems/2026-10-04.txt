I love the quiet way you make the restless world feel still,
a steady, gentle presence that no bitter wind can chill.
Thank you for your patient heart, your listening, open mind,
and for the tender ways you choose to constantly be kind.
In you, I found a safe retreat, a place where I belong,
an honest grace that softly turns the darkness into song.
So when the road is steep ahead and shadows make you small,
remember all the strength you bear to rise above it all.
Go chase the dreams that call your name, step proudly into light,
and know that I am by your side through every day and night.
