You bring a steady calm into my loudest days,
a quiet grace in how you listen, how you stay.
I see how deeply you can love, how much you give,
and how your gentle spirit makes it good to live.
I hope you know the quiet wonders that you do,
the lighter air around me just from being you.
On days the heavy world begins to blur your light,
trust in the strength you carry, silent and upright.
You are more than enough, in all you face and start,
and you will always have a haven in my heart.
