You have a way of softening the edges of the day,
of making room for hope whenever colors turn to gray.
I love the quiet beauty of the heart you never hide,
and all the steady comfort found in walking by your side.
Thank you for the patience that you give without a sound,
for being such an anchor when the world is spinning round.
Whenever doubt creeps near you and your spirit starts to tire,
remember you are stronger than the things the days require.
You are more deeply cherished than these gentle lines can show,
and through every storm or sunrise, I hope you always know.
