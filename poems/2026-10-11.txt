You have a way of softening the world,
turning the loudest days to steady ground.
I love the quiet kindness in your hands,
the honest, patient grace in how you care.
Thank you for simply being who you are—
a home I never have to second-guess.
And when the path ahead feels steep or dark,
remember how much light you carry through;
you are stronger than the shadows in your mind,
and I am always here, believing in you.
