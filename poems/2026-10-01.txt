I see the quiet grace in how you move through heavy days,
The gentle way you lend your light without the need for praise.
You’ve turned the sharpest corners of my world to somewhere kind,
A steady harbor for my heart, the sweetest peace I find.
So when the shadows stretch too long and you forget your worth,
Remember you are among the rarest gifts upon this earth.
You carry so much quiet strength behind your tired eyes,
More resilient than the winter winds, more boundless than the skies.
Just take a breath and trust your stride; you do not walk alone—
Beside you in the storm or sun, you’ll always have a home.
