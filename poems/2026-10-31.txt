You bring a quiet comfort to the noise,
A steady warmth that turns the cold away.
I love the gentle kindness in your voice,
And how you find the beauty in the day.
When heavy shadows try to dim your light,
Remember all the grace inside your chest;
You are far stronger than the longest night,
More capable than what your doubts suggest.
Through every storm, across whatever sea,
You have my whole heart, walking next to me.
