You have a quiet way of softening the world,
of turning heavy days into something light and kind.
I don’t say it enough, but I notice everything—
the patience in your voice, the grace you leave behind.
Thank you for being the shelter where I feel completely safe,
for understanding pieces of me I struggle to explain.
On the days when doubt creeps in and shadows start to grow,
remember the fierce and gentle light you carry through the rain.
You are capable of more than you ever give yourself credit for,
and whatever comes next, I am right here by your side.
