You have a way of softening the world,
Just by existing—gentle, steady, true.
Whenever heavy thoughts are all unfurled,
I find my anchor simply being with you.
I see the quiet kindness that you share,
The patient heart that gives without a sound,
And on the days that feel too much to bear,
I hope you feel the strength you keep around.
Walk forward softly, proud of what you do;
The world is brighter just because of you.
