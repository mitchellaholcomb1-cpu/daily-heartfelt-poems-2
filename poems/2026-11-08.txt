You have a quiet way of making the world feel soft,
of turning weary hours into something I can hold.
I love the steady kindness in the way you listen,
and the brave, honest heart you carry every day.
Whenever doubt begins to cloud your view,
I wish you could see yourself through my eyes—
not just as the refuge you are to everyone else,
but as someone fierce, rare, and deeply deserving of joy.
Whatever lies ahead, remember your own strength;
I believe in you completely, and I am always by your side.
