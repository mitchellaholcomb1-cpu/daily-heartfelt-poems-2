You bring a quiet warmth to hurried days,
a steady grace that never asks to shine.
I love the gentle honesty you give,
the way your presence softens even mine.
Thank you for being shelter, spark, and home,
for sharing all the goodness in your soul.
On days the road feels heavy in your hands,
remember you are resilient, brave, and whole.
Keep stepping forward, beautiful and true;
there is no light quite like the one in you.
