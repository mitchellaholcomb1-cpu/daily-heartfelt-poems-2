In every crowded room, you are the quiet grace,
the steady warmth that turns an unfamiliar place to home.
I love the honest kindness in the things you do,
the gentle way you listen when the day is long,
and how the world grows softer just by having you.
On mornings when you doubt the strength inside your chest,
remember that you carry more than you can see.
You make this life a better place by being here,
and through whatever storms or seasons come your way,
you’ll always have a steady, faithful home in me.
