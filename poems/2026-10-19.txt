You make the heavy corners of the world feel light,
simply by being the steady, gentle soul you are.
I see the quiet grace in everything you do,
the patience you give so freely without keeping score.
Thank you for being my calm when the days run wild,
and for bringing such effortless warmth into my life.
On the mornings when you doubt your own bright worth,
remember the quiet strength you carry in your chest.
You are loved more deeply than these lines can say,
and wherever tomorrow leads, you will never walk alone.
