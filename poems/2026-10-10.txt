In the quiet corners of my everyday,
you are the steady warmth that never fades.
Thank you for the gentle grace you bring,
and for the rare, sweet gift of being known.
On days when shadows linger on your path,
remember all the light you carry through;
you have a quiet strength that meets the storm,
and deeper courage than you ever claim.
Trust in your heart, wherever it may lead,
and know I’m here, believing in your stride.
