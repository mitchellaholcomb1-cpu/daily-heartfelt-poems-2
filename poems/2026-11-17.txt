You bring a quiet, steady warmth into my days,
a gentleness that softens every hurried hour.
I love the way you listen, how you simply are,
turning the most ordinary corners into home.
When the world asks too much and the climb feels steep,
remember the fierce, beautiful strength you carry within.
You never have to brave the cold or doubt alone;
my hand is always here, steady in your own.
Thank you for the light you give without even trying,
and for being the truest, safest gift in my life.
