The world grows gentler every time you walk into the room,
a sudden warmth that cuts across the winter cold.
I love the way your quiet courage breaks the gloom,
and how your patient hands make frayed things whole.
Thank you for simply being everything you are—
a steady grace, a harbor through the pouring rain.
On days you feel unseen, or like the climb is far,
remember you are stronger than your deepest pain.
Keep trusting in your light, the way I always do;
there is no one on earth quite as rare and dear as you.
