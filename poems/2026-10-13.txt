You have a way of making heavy days feel light,
A quiet grace that turns the darkest hours bright.
I see how hard you try, the gentle care you give,
And how you make this world a better place to live.
When doubt creeps in and whispers that you might not be enough,
Remember you are woven out of strong and steady stuff.
You carry so much courage in the way you softly stand;
I hope you know I’ll always be right here to hold your hand.
Through every turn and season that the coming days impart,
You are the truest, warmest home inside my heart.
