In a world that moves too fast, you are my quiet ground,
the safest, warmest harbor that my heart has found.
I love the gentle way you listen when I speak,
and how your sudden laughter makes a hard day sweet.
Thank you for simply being everything you are—
a steady, patient light beneath an unsettled sky.
When heavy shadows fall and doubts begin to rise,
remember all the quiet strength you carry deep inside.
You are so capable, so brave, so endlessly true,
and through every step ahead, I’m right here believing in you.
