You carry a quiet light wherever you go,
softening the edges of the heaviest days,
and I wonder if you see the grace you bestow
just by moving through the world in your honest way.
I love the rare, sudden bloom of your smile,
and the brave, tender patience you keep within.
Whenever the road feels too steep for a while,
remember the quiet resilience beneath your skin.
Keep reaching for the life you deserve to hold;
I am here, always grateful, watching you unfold.
