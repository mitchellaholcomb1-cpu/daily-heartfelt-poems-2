You have a way of making heavy days feel light,
a quiet warmth that turns the darkest room to dawn.
I am so grateful for the simple grace you carry,
and the gentle shelter of having you to lean upon.
I love the steady, honest beauty of your heart,
the patient way you care, the kindness that you sow.
So when the world forgets to be as good as you are,
and whispers doubts about how far you still can go,
remember who you are beneath the noise and shadow—
you are deeply cherished, and braver than you know.
