You carry a quiet, steady grace
that softens the world without asking to be seen,
and I don’t think you know how much brighter the days are
simply because you share them with me.
When the road feels steep and your own strength falters,
remember the boundless wonder of who you are,
and how naturally you turn cold, uncertain spaces
into a place that feels like home.
Keep going, with that gentle heart of yours;
you have a light no shadow can ever take,
and through every season that is yet to come,
you will never have to walk alone.
