You have a way of making the quiet feel like home,
a gentle light that softens even the heaviest day.
I see how much heart you pour into this world,
the steady grace you carry, even when you're tired.
On the days when doubt whispers in the background,
I hope you remember the quiet power within your bones.
You are stronger than the storms you navigate,
and more precious to me than easy words can hold.
Walk forward gently, trusting where your feet land;
I will be right here, believing in you, every step of the way.
