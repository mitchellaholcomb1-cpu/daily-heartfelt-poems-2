You bring a gentle, steady sort of peace
That makes the heavy, rushed world softly cease.
I love how you listen, the way that you care,
The warmth in the room just because you are there.
On days when you carry more weight than you should,
Remember your power, your truth, and your good.
You don’t have to prove what is already true:
The world is far brighter, far better with you.
So take the next step without fear in your stride;
I’m cheering you on, always right by your side.
