In a world that so often rushes past,
your presence is the quiet where I breathe.
I see the steady kindness that you offer,
the way you hold the heavy things with grace,
and how you make the simple days feel sacred.
When tomorrow asks too much of you,
remember the brave light you carry inside—
a strength you rarely praise, but always own.
Keep walking with your chin held to the sky;
you are so deeply loved, exactly as you are.
