You bring a quiet shelter from the cold,
a steady grace in everything you do.
Of all the gentle gifts this life can hold,
my favorite place will always be with you.
Thank you for the beauty of your heart,
and the light you give away so selflessly.
Whenever doubt creeps in to do its part,
remember all the strength in you I see.
Keep reaching for the dreams you hold so close;
you are braver than you know, and deeply loved.
