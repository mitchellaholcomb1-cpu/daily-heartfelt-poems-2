I love the quiet shelter of your laugh,
and how you turn the greyest day into a home.
Thank you for the steady patience in your heart,
for seeing me completely and still holding close.
Whenever doubt begins to lean against your shoulders,
remember just how luminous and brave you are.
You do not have to carry all the dark alone;
I’ll be right here to walk beside you, come what may.
You make this spinning world a softer place to breathe,
and loving you is the truest thing I know.
