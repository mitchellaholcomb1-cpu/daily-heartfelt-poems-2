The world is loud, but you are steady ground,
the gentlest kind of grace I’ve ever found.
I see the quiet battles that you fight,
and how you turn the shadows into light.
Thank you for being home inside my chest,
for loving me when I am not my best.
On days you doubt the magic that you hold,
remember you are rare, and brave, and gold.
Keep going, love, through whatever comes your way;
I believe in you completely, every single day.
