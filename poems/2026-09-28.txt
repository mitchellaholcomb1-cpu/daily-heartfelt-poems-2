You carry so much quiet light without even trying,
a gentle warmth that softens my hardest days.
I don’t tell you enough how deeply I see you—
your steady courage, your patience, the kindness you give away.
When the world demands too much and you doubt your own power,
I hope you remember the safe harbor waiting in my arms.
Take a breath, trust yourself, and keep moving forward;
you are so much stronger than the shadows that test you,
and no matter how far or steep the climb becomes,
you will never have to walk a single step alone.
