You bring a quiet kindness everywhere you go,
a steady warmth that makes the heavy days feel light.
I don’t think you always see how much you glow,
or how your gentle courage cuts right through the night.
Thank you for the patient ways you listen and care,
for being a peaceful harbor when the world is loud.
When doubt begins to whisper that the road is bare,
remember your own strength, the grace that makes me proud.
Keep moving toward your morning with hope in your stride,
and know that you are cherished, with me right by your side.
